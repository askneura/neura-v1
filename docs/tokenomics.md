# Tokenomics

The planned NEURA supply, allocation shares and team vesting.

> Reference documentation. V1 contracts are unaudited and not deployed. Read the [security limitations](../SECURITY.md); descriptions are not guarantees.

Neura plans a fixed total supply of **60,000,000 $NEURA**. No tokens have been minted, sold or distributed yet, and no token price, valuation or circulating supply has been announced.

## Allocation plan

| Share | Percent | Tokens | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ecosystem & growth | 35% | 21,000,000 | Proposed |
| Protocol treasury | 20% | 12,000,000 | Proposed |
| Community | 18% | 10,800,000 | Confirmed plan |
| Marketing & partnerships | 12% | 7,200,000 | Proposed |
| Liquidity pools | 10% | 6,000,000 | Confirmed plan |
| Team | 5% | 3,000,000 | Confirmed plan |

**Confirmed plan** shares are fixed in the allocation plan. **Proposed** shares describe the intended direction; they are not approved, and the final split may change before any distribution.

## What each share is for

- **Community (18%)** — early participation and community programs, including Check OG eligibility. Check OG records eligibility only; the final program breakdown and claim mechanics are not yet published.
- **Liquidity pools (10%)** — reserved for liquidity provisioning. Pool venues, paired assets and custody or lock arrangements will be disclosed separately. A token reserve alone is not funded liquidity.
- **Ecosystem & growth (proposed)** — builders, integrations, liquidity incentives and long-term protocol growth. Programs and release schedules require approval before distribution.
- **Protocol treasury (proposed)** — a long-term reserve for development, security reviews, operations and resilience.
- **Marketing & partnerships (proposed)** — education, community reach and ecosystem partnerships.
- **Team (5%)** — compensation, locked and vested as below.

## Team vesting

The team's 3,000,000 $NEURA stay **fully locked for the first year**. After the lock, tokens release **linearly over five years** — 50,000 per month equivalent. There is no lump-sum payout at the end of the lock. Half the team allocation is vested at year 3.5; all of it at year 6.

This is a vesting commitment in the plan, **not an existing on-chain lock**. The start date, contract addresses and verification links will be announced before implementation.

## What is not yet decided

- Release schedules for non-team shares and the initial circulating supply
- Claim mechanics and any minting or supply controls
- Token deployment, independent security review and treasury custody
- Any governance rights
