# FAQ

Short answers to common questions.

> Reference documentation. V1 contracts are unaudited and not deployed. Read the [security limitations](../SECURITY.md); descriptions are not guarantees.

**Is Neura custodial?** No. You sign everything in your own wallet.

**Is there a Neura token?** Not today. Check OG allocations are not tokens.

**When do Swap and Pools launch?** After audits. Follow [@AskNeura](https://x.com/AskNeura).

**Why did my swap fail?** Usually the price moved past your slippage, the deadline passed, or you lack gas.

**Can fees change after I deposit?** No. A pool's fee tier is fixed forever.

**Why did a second trade in the same block cost more?** That is the [surge fee](fees.md), which protects you from sandwich bots.

**Which pool should I use for stablecoins?** The 0.01% stable pool.

**Who can I contact?** [X](https://x.com/AskNeura) or [Telegram](https://t.me/AskNeura). Neura staff will never DM you first or ask for your seed phrase.
