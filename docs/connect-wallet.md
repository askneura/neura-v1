# Connect a wallet

Supported wallets, the one-time ownership signature and auto-reconnect.

> Reference documentation. V1 contracts are unaudited and not deployed. Read the [security limitations](../SECURITY.md); descriptions are not guarantees.

Your wallet is your only account on Neura — no email, no password.

## Steps

1. Press **Connect Wallet**. On desktop pick a browser extension or scan the QR code; on mobile, your wallet app (MetaMask, OKX, Trust, Rainbow, Coinbase…) opens directly.
2. Tick the Terms and Privacy boxes and **sign one gas-free message**. This proves you own the address. It is not a transaction and cannot move funds.
3. Done. Neura remembers the approval and reconnects you automatically next time.

## Disconnecting

Use **Disconnect** in the wallet menu. Auto-reconnect stays off until you connect again.

> Never sign a message that asks for your seed phrase, or a transaction you do not understand. Neura will only ever ask for the readable agreement message.
