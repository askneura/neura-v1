# Check OG

How the one-time Neura Originals allocation works.

> Reference documentation. V1 contracts are unaudited and not deployed. Read the [security limitations](../SECURITY.md); descriptions are not guarantees.

Check OG reviews a verified wallet's public on-chain history across all six networks and records a **Neura allocation**.

## What is considered

- Liquidity provision on DEXs
- Holdings over time
- Trading and wallet activity
- Wallet age and multi-chain use

## Rules

- **One wallet, one check.** The result is final and stored permanently; it cannot be re-rolled, edited or deleted.
- You must sign the ownership message first.
- After checking, link X, follow @AskNeura and join Telegram, then register (optionally with a referral code).

An allocation is a record of eligibility, **not a token** and not a promise of value.
